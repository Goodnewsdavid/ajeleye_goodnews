01 — About

The creator
and the face.

I am a UGC video creator and a model. Brands hire me when they want someone who can hold the camera, wear the look, and still sound like a real girl sharing a favorite find not an actress reading a script.

I write, film, and appear in the work: talking-head reviews, getting-ready videos, unboxings, and campaign stills. You get scroll-stopping footage and a face that already belongs in the brand.

02 — What I do

UGC videos

Short-form video for TikTok, Reels, and paid social: talking to camera, unboxings, GRWM, reviews, and hooks written to feel native. Raw or lightly cut, ready to run.

Modeling

On-camera talent and lifestyle modeling for the same campaigns. Lookbooks, product-on-body stills, and face-of-the-brand frames that match the videos.
